# Irixi 办公 Agent｜第一轮原创概念图 prompts

生成方式：Codex 内置图像生成工具。

这一轮是美术方向概念，不是已切分的 `64×72 px` 生产精灵。确认方向后，再基于定装角色制作透明基准帧、关键姿势和逐帧动画。

## 1. 角色四向定装

Use case: stylized-concept

Create an entirely original Victorian anthropomorphic owl gentleman named Irixi. He has a heart-shaped ivory facial disc, expressive amber eyes, a small dark beak, strong brow feathers, broad shoulders, a fitted deep forest-green tailcoat, burgundy bow tie, parchment waistcoat, dark trousers, short leather boots, brass details, a tall Victorian top hat, a slender ebony walking cane with a brass owl-head handle, and a delicate monocle with a visible chain attached to his waistcoat.

Show exactly four complete full-body views of the same character: front-left three-quarter, front-right three-quarter, back-left three-quarter, and back-right three-quarter. Keep identical height, proportions, clothing, palette, accessories, and foot baseline. The monocle stays on the character's anatomical right eye and the cane stays in the anatomical left hand. Draw each direction independently; do not mirror.

Authentic hand-crafted pixel art, crisp hard-edged pixels, no anti-aliasing, painterly rendering, or 3D. Design each figure as if it occupies a transparent `64×72 px` sprite cell, with the character approximately 50–54 px tall. Palette: deep walnut, antique brass, forest green, burgundy, parchment, and cool gray. Entirely original; no copied mascot, sprite skeleton, logo, label, text, or watermark.

## 2. 六种任务状态

Use case: stylized-concept

Create a six-pose concept sheet featuring exactly the same original Victorian anthropomorphic owl gentleman in every panel. Preserve the heart-shaped ivory facial disc, amber eyes, forest-green tailcoat, burgundy bow tie, parchment waistcoat, tall top hat, anatomical-right monocle and anatomical-left cane ownership.

Arrange six isolated full-body vignettes in a clean `2×3` grid: reading an open dossier; drafting with a quill at a walnut desk while the cane is parked in a brass stand; checking two documents through the monocle; waiting for approval beside a sealed submission folder; completing work with a decisive stamp and restrained hat-tip; reacting to an ink-blotted error sheet with a small recoil and ruffled neck feathers. Keep identical scale and readable occupational body language.

Authentic hand-crafted pixel art with crisp hard-edged pixels. No costume drift, mirrored accessories, extra limbs, cropping, labels, logos, UI, or watermark. Entirely original and unrelated to any existing office-agent artwork.

## 3. 八角档案厅

Use case: stylized-concept

Create an entirely original 1890s Victorian corner-office miniature architectural cutaway in a `16:9` composition. Use a roughly 30-degree three-quarter orthographic elevated view with verticals kept vertical. Center the architecture on a double-height octagonal archive hall with an octagonal walnut archive island, surrounding gallery, curved aged-brass railings, tall archive shelves, rolling ladder, asymmetrical staircase, and circular owl-eye roof lantern.

Use deep walnut, aged brass, forest-green wallpaper and leather, burgundy upholstery, parchment, and cool gray stone. Contrast restrained amber lamp pools with cool roof daylight. Authentic pixel art with crisp clusters and hard shadows. Avoid generic open-plan offices, repeated cubicles, checkerboard flooring, strict diamond-map isometry, modern furniture, cyberpunk, and gratuitous steampunk gears. No text, logo, label, watermark, character, or borrowed room layout.

## 5. 小动物同事候选阵容

Use the Irixi turnaround only as a reference for palette, pixel density, and Victorian tailoring. Do not copy the owl anatomy, face, beak, feathers, silhouette, or top-hat/monocle/cane combination.

Create exactly eight distinct lovable small-animal office colleagues in a `4×2` full-body lineup: a golden hamster with ledger; a soft gray field mouse with rolled document; a charcoal velvet mole with brass folding ruler; a caramel dormouse with teacup; a silver-gray chinchilla with blueprint tube; a sand-gold jerboa with pocket watch; a cream-and-caramel guinea pig with document satchel; and an elegant black-and-white fancy rat with sealing stamp.

Give each animal a different silhouette, Victorian clothing construction, and one small office prop. Keep them rounded, clean, warm, intelligent, and gently comic without becoming infantile. Absolutely no owl or bird anatomy, and do not give everyone top hats, monocles, or canes. Avoid vermin stereotypes, red eyes, exposed teeth, dirt, disease, scars, sewer imagery, bald-tail closeups, horror, text, labels, logos, or watermarks.

## 6. 唯一猫头鹰主角与小动物团队

Use the Irixi turnaround as the exact identity reference for the one and only owl protagonist, the animal lineup as the supporting-cast reference, and the octagonal office as the architecture and lighting reference.

Surround him with different small-animal colleagues such as a drafting hamster, archive-organizing mole, document-checking field mouse, blueprint-carrying chinchilla, waiting guinea pig, and stamping fancy rat. Each colleague keeps its own species silhouette, outfit, and prop.

There must be no other owl or bird. Supporting animals must not inherit beaks, facial discs, feathers, wings, bird feet, or the owl's signature combination of top hat, monocle, and cane. Preserve the walnut, brass, forest-green, burgundy, parchment, and cool-gray visual language; crisp pixel clusters; and the octagonal archive architecture. Avoid modern objects, generic cubicles, sewer imagery, ugly vermin stereotypes, text, labels, UI, logos, or watermarks.

## 7. 小动物生产比例测试

Use the approved animal lineup as the exact cast identity source. Redraw the same eight characters in a `4×2` grid as deliberately simplified low-resolution production sprites. Treat every figure as if authored directly inside a fixed transparent `64×72 px` logical cell, with a 50–54 px-tall visible character, shared foot baseline, large transparent gutters, complete props, and no cell borders.

Use large pixel clusters, a warm one-logical-pixel outline, roughly 12–16 colors per character, and only 2–3 shading levels per material. Enlarge eyes, hands, and the single signature prop slightly for readability. Preserve the hamster cheeks, mouse ears, mole paws, dormouse tail, chinchilla cloud silhouette, jerboa legs, tailless guinea-pig body, and black-and-white fancy-rat pattern. No gradients, soft fur strands, antialiasing, costume drift, duplicated species, owl features, text, UI, logo, or watermark.
